CC0 recordings: Sam Gossner, Simon Dalzell, Versilian Studios and VCSL contributors.
Sources: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE and https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
Mono 24kHz PCM16 prototype, sustain/staccato, two available velocity layers, one round robin.
No recorded legato transitions or accent samples. Euphonium, alto sax and baritone sax are NOT present.
Pitch convention of original filenames: C3=60. See provenance.json for pinned commits/checksums.
Bundling removes only PCM beyond enabled sustain loops (plus interpolation guard frames); retained PCM is byte-identical. Original converted checksums remain in provenance alongside final packaged checksums.
