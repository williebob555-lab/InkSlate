## Findings

### Data loss and wrong state

**T3-01 · S1 · Teaching a page turn deletes every recording of the song.** Settings > Listen on, a recording plays, the player turns page 1 -> 2 by hand -> the song's whole `audio` list becomes empty (label, speed, loop, click tempo, volume all gone; the log line still says "learned the turn"). Cause: `Listener.pageChanged` does `editSong(id) { audio = audio.orEmpty().map { ... } }`; inside `editSong` the name `audio` is the edit's own (null) field, not the song's, so it writes `[]`. It syncs. A later folder scan may re-pair some files by name, with settings lost; self-recorded takes and hand-paired ones (names that do not start with the title) are not re-found. Evidence: `T3ListenTest.teaching a page turn erases every recording of the song` ("recordings = []"). The "learn by turning" path cannot work in this build. Status: open (check whether the main checkout's PlayAlong.kt still reads `audio` inside the edit block).

**T3-02 · S2 · Listen and the play button read a stale copy of the song.** `state.current` is set when a song comes to the front and never refreshed when the library changes (turns taught, a recording paired or re-ordered, speed changed from another device). `Listener.start` and `Recording.toggle` read `state.current.audio`. Taught turns written to the library were invisible to Listen ("library=[10000,20000,30000], state.current=[]"); pairing a recording while a song is open leaves the strip's Play doing nothing until the song is reopened. Evidence: `T3ListenTest.things edited after a song came to the front...`. Status: open.

**T3-03 · S2 · Turns are stored per recording, shared by every part of the song (confirmed by you; evidence for completeness).** Score (5 pages) taught 30/60/90/120 s; then a 2-page part's one turn at 70 s overwrites turn 1 -> 70/60/90/120, out of order. A 2-page part is given the Score's turn 1 (30 s) as its own. Glancing at page 2 while a recording happens to play rewrites turn 1. Out-of-order turns made Listen turn **twice within 0.1 s** (30.1 s -> p2, 30.2 s -> p3). Evidence: `T3CoreTest.turn plan - one recording, two parts...`, `T3ListenTest.two parts...`, `...turn twice in a row`. Teaching goes to the track that is *playing* (`Recording.track`) but Listen follows `audio.first()`, so turns taught on a second recording are never used (`...Listen follows the first recording, not the one that was playing`). Playing a recording never turns a page by itself (answer to the lead's question: `turnsMs` is read only by Listener, i.e. only with the microphone; test `playing a recording never turns a page by itself`).

**T3-04 · S2 · A missed lesson leaves `-1` and Listen turns the page the moment it starts.** Turning 2->3 first while a recording plays stores `[-1, 40000]`; `TurnPlan.learned` counts it as complete and `turnAt(0)` is -1, so Listen turned page 1->2 at 0.4 s of hearing, and "Turned to page 1" was shown (the page it left). Evidence: `...learned turns with a minus one...`.

**T3-05 · S2 · A take is unplayable if the app dies while recording yourself.** `WavWriter` writes the length only at close(); the app's own player refuses a header with length 0 (`load=false`) although the comment promises "most players work out the length". Evidence: `T3RecordingTest.a take killed mid-write...` (4 s take copied before close: load=false; closed: 4000 ms). The take is also not paired with the song until stop, so a crash leaves an orphan wav. Fix: write the header at start and refresh it every few seconds; pair at start.

**T3-06 · S2 · Two devices changing different things on a song's recordings lose one change; two devices each recording a take lose one take.** `audio` is one last-writer-wins field: tablet taught turns on "Band" while the laptop slowed "Me": both devices end with the laptop's list, no turns. Each device adding a take: one take disappears. Evidence: `T3EdgeTest.two devices...`. (Same class as T1-04.)

### Listen

**T3-17 · S1/S2 · The tuner or recording yourself takes the microphone from Listen and nothing says so.** The desktop and Android microphones are one line: `start()` stops the previous listener, `stop()` stops all. Opening and closing the Tuner (or "Record yourself", or Settings Test) silenced Listen: mic log `[stop,start,stop,start,stop]`, Listen still "active", `Ears.deaf` false, the button frozen at "Turn in 19s" / "Turning..." for ever. Silent failure, which PREFERENCES forbids. Fix: route tuner and recorder through `Ears` (ref-counted) and make `deaf` also trip when no chunk has arrived for 2 s. Evidence: `T3ListenTest.the tuner or a self recording takes the microphone...`, `T3RecordingTest.recording yourself takes the microphone from Listen...`.

**T3-18 · S2 · Listen has no trust when you start mid-piece or on the wrong page.** Forward-only per-page rule. Simulated (60 s recording, turns 15/30/45 s):
 - band at 32 s (page 3), Listen pressed on page 1: the button counts "Turn in 14s ... 2s" for 12 s while the band is already past it, then flips p2, p3, p4 within 0.5 s.
 - band at 5 s (page 1), Listen pressed on page 3: it turns **forward** to page 4 at 10.6 s (a false turn onto the last page), never goes back.
 - band restarts from the top at 28 s (rehearsal restart): a false turn to the last page 3 s later (position extrapolated before the follower re-aligns; it needs 5 s of sound), then the last page stays for the whole second pass ("Last page - following").
 Only pressing it on the right page at the start works (band at 20 s, page 2: correct turn). Evidence: `T3ListenTest.Listen pressed while the band is already part way...`, `...starts again from the top...`.

**T3-19 · S2 · Four seconds of quiet ends Listen for good.** `MusicPresence(quietForMs = 4000)`: 5 s of silence at 20 s of a 50 s piece (a fermata, a tacet bar, the director talking) -> "The music stopped", Listen off, the remaining turns missed, no re-arm. In rehearsal the band stops every 20-60 s. Evidence: `...a four second silence...`.

**T3-20 · S3 · The gauge shows the level, not what it hears.** It shows microphone level, countdown, "Page 2 of 5", "turns guessed". It does not show where it thinks the band is (bar/time), how sure, or what it matched; the level bar moves for any sound (talking, another band). The user's "no clue what it's hearing" is accurate. At phone width the card makes the strip a second column, and with the playback column a third (`phone-t3-listen-deaf.png`); the dead-mic device name is truncated ("Microphone Array (Intel" - UiAudit TRUNCATED at all three sizes). "Turned to page N" names the page it left for about a second.

**T3-21 · S3 · Listen is gated three ways and explained in a paragraph.** Needs Settings > Experimental, a recording (or music read) per song, and a taught or guessed turn list; guessed turns spread pages evenly over the recording, the least likely way pages fall. The microphone choice and Test meter only appear with Listen on, though the Tuner and Record yourself use the same input.

**T3-22 · S3 · Failure to open the microphone says "The microphone could not be opened" for 5 s whatever the reason** (Android: permission not yet granted -> the first press only asks, the second works; no hint). Evidence: `...microphone that cannot be opened...`.

### Tempo follow, metronome, tap tempo

**T3-23 · S2 · Following the band's tempo depends on where the metronome started, and the click is muted anyway.** Real recordings through the tracker as TempoFollow does: Sweet Caroline from 100 -> 136, from 90 -> 68 (half of 136) and stays; September from 100 -> 128, from 60 -> 52..64 for 100 s; Don't Stop Believin' from 150 needs 60 s; a bare 112 bpm kick from 60 locks at 57 (all raw readings off). Near starts converge in 6-8 s. (The recordings' true tempos were not independently known: the evidence is the start-dependence, not absolute error.) `TempoFollow` mutes the click, so there is nothing audible to follow with; and `Click.begin` calls `reset()` which clears `muted`, so Start or Count in while following makes the click audible again and the microphone hears it (`T3CoreTest.metronome - a count-in cue ... reset un-mutes`). Evidence: `build/t3out/tempo.txt`.

**T3-24 · S3 · Tap tempo is not robust.** Four taps at 120: one late tap gives 100; a contact bounce gives 125. Use the median gap. (`T3CoreTest.tap tempo`.)

**T3-25 · S3 · Metronome offers 2/3/4/6 beats only, unlabeled subdivision chips, +/-1 only.** No 5, 7, 9, 12 or accent pattern (the engine supports `accents`). Timing itself is exact (within 0.001 ms per beat at 20-300 bpm, no drift) - solid.

**T3-26 · S4 · Tuner is accurate but minimal.** Tones from bass E1 (41 Hz) to trombone F5: all within 0.5 cents, 33 of 33 shown; a chord reads as one low note at clarity 0.99; 1.9 ms per detection on the laptop every ~14 ms (a phone may be slower - measure on the Tab). No drone/reference pitch (`Tuner.hzOf` is unused), A= resets each time, names follow the first instrument of the profile, not the part showing.

**T3-27 · S3 · The tuner's and recorder's microphone cannot be chosen without switching on an experimental feature** (see T3-21).

### Watch

**T3-28 · S2 · The watch is never told the phone is no longer in use.** `WatchFlicks.inUseNow()` is `remote.connected || state.currentPath != null` and `currentPath` is never set back to null (grep: no `currentPath = null`), so once any part has been opened the phone beats "listen" every 60 s for the life of the process: closing every part, going Home or locking the screen changes nothing (no lifecycle hook in the Android app code at all). The watch stops only after `QUIET_MS` = 3 min without a beat (checked every 30 s: up to 3.5 min), holding a partial wake lock and the gyroscope meanwhile, then buzzes. If the process is killed: same wait. Want: stop when the last part closes, when the screen turns off or the app stops, with one short buzz.

**T3-29 · S2 · A flick the phone cannot answer buzzes a long 0.7 s per flick.** `FlickService.failed` -> `buzz(700 ms)` on every send failure or 1.5 s without answer, no rate limit, no give-up state: a watch out of range, a closed app or a phone asleep gives a long buzz on each flick while it keeps listening. Should: one short double tick, then quiet until the link is back.

**T3-30 · S2 · A weak calibration is still activated and sent to the watch.** `Calibration.finish` activates `r.model` whenever there is one, even with `problem != null`; the done screen says "In use now - the watch has it." above the warning. Made-up arms: playing x4: margin 2.05, report clean, yet 3 false turns in 10 min of 1.4x wilder playing; x6: margin 1.12, 19 false turns; x8: 201 (held-out cues 6 right, 1 wrong, 3 missed). A false turn on stage is worse than a missed one. The threshold is one fixed number from one 3-minute session. (Simulation; real arms will differ - the point is the model is not guarded.) Evidence: `T3CoreTest.flicks - calibration quality...`.

**T3-31 · S3 · Calibration names with punctuation mismatch their folder** ("Bass (Fender)" is stored as `Bass _Fender_`, listed by the folder name while `active` and instrument assignments use the typed name), so "in use" and "For ..." labels can disagree (read from `WatchFlicks`/`safe()`, not run). Settings has a 4-line paragraph per experimental switch; calibrating is 3+ minutes with no resume.

### Play together over Bluetooth (read + MeshFrames tests)

**T3-32 · S3 · Two messages in the same second share an id and the second never shows.** `noteId = note.at / 1000`; receivers de-duplicate on `(noteId, index)` and `NoteAssembler.done`. A leader tapping two presets quickly loses the second (`T3CoreTest.mesh frames - messages sent in the same second`).

**T3-33 · S3 · Over 150 bytes is silently cut** (400 characters arrive as 150, no marker, leader not told). 16-bit session id from the leader's name: leaders with the same device name ("Galaxy Tab S9 FE") share a session. A restarted leader (seq back to 1) is ignored by a follower that saw a higher seq unless it passed 10 000. `songKey` needs the identical title. Random byte strings: 15 of 5000 decode as frames (no crash). 0xFE5A is a SIG-assigned member UUID: confirm it is not another company's.

**T3-34 · S3 · `BleMeshRadio` read-through (not run):** scan is LOW_LATENCY and never stops while the switch is on (battery); the permission request returns false, so the first toggle only asks and the user must toggle again with only "Waiting for Bluetooth permission" in Settings; relaying happens only on devices that are following; frames rotate every 250 ms through state plus all note pieces, so a 15-piece message delays the page position by up to ~4 s. On desktop Settings says "Not available on this device" under a switch that is still tappable.

## Making it everyday

### Listen and turn pages
Use: rehearsal with both hands on the instrument and nobody to flip - the director restarts bars, stops to talk, jumps to letters. Not a stage tool until it never turns wrongly.
Stops me: T3-01/02 (teaching breaks), T3-17 (silently dead mic), T3-18/19 (start mid-piece, restarts, a 4 s pause), no sense of where it thinks we are, three prerequisites and Settings.
Make it everyday:
1. **Join anywhere:** on start and every re-sync search the whole piece (the follower already can) and put the page up *or down* to match; show "Bar 36, page 3, 80% sure" and a mini page strip with the heard position; replace the level bar by a heard-vs-expected line.
2. **Rehearsal mode:** stay armed through pauses (no 4 s stop); "Paused - waiting", resume on the next sound; arm per song or per set, not per press.
3. **Never turn on low confidence:** amber "unsure" and do nothing; one-tap "turn now" and learn from corrections (a manual turn during Listen means "wrong").
4. **Turns per part and per page taught with one play-through** instead of an even-spread guess; or from the music read. Store bar numbers so another recording or tempo reuses them.
5. **One microphone owner:** tuner, recorder, Test, Listen and tempo follow through `Ears`; say who has it ("Tuner has the microphone; Listen paused").
6. Mic choice and Test in Settings > Sound for everyone; graduate Listen out of Experimental when 1-5 are done.

### Tempo follow
Use: "are we rushing?" and counting off at the band's tempo, rather than driving a click. Stops me: the click is muted, so it does nothing audible; it locks at half-time; it ignores the song's printed tempo.
Make it everyday: a **tempo readout with rushing/dragging against the song's marked tempo** (no click), "Set song tempo to this", "Count off at the band's tempo". Seed the tracker from the song's tempo, not the metronome's last value, and test half/double explicitly. Starting the click while following must unmute or refuse clearly.

### Watch flicks
Use: on stage, hands full, right wrist. Stops me: no guard against false turns, long buzzing, stays on after I close the part or lock the phone.
Make it everyday: (1) **arm by context** - listen only while a part is open *and* the phone screen is on, stop when the last part closes (T3-28) with one short buzz; (2) a **two-flick option** per instrument for stage, single flick for rehearsal; (3) **undo** - a flick back within 2 s after a turn is an undo, and a flick the phone cannot answer is one short tick then quiet (T3-29); (4) refuse to activate a weak calibration (T3-30) and offer "30 s more playing" instead of a full redo; (5) an "Armed" dot on the strip so I know when it is live; (6) a separate "lively" profile for marches.

### Play together over Bluetooth
Use: eduroam, a gym with no Wi-Fi; the director's page and messages reach 50 phones. Stops me: Android only, buried in Settings, every device needs it, a first toggle that only asks for permission, same-name leaders.
Make it everyday: turn on **automatically** when Play together cannot connect for a few seconds ("Wi-Fi is blocking us - use Bluetooth?"); "12 devices in range" for the leader (a count, no flood); a random per-leader session id carried in the QR/join link; message length shown with a "cut" marker; idle devices relay.

### Metronome, tuner, recordings, practice log, notes
- **Metronome:** odd meters and an accent pattern in the dialog, one-tap "Song tempo", robust tap, a visual beat on the screen edge, count-off that starts the recording on the first beat (works).
- **Tuner:** reference drone/tone and "tune to Bb" for open notes; remember A=; names per the part's instrument; same screen on the remote.
- **Recordings:** an always-visible transport (play, speed, A-B loop, "from letter C") instead of -5/+5 only; compress takes (m4a/opus) and trim the count-in; auto-title takes by date and part; a take button on the Remote.
- **Practice log:** wire `addPractice` to recording playback, the metronome and Listen; "This week: 3 h 20, September 45 min" on the song's details.
- **Notes / reminders:** a reminder as a note beside the page, once per set run; "Done" -> "Remove".
