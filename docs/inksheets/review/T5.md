## Playback: musical judgement

`T5PlaybackTest` (56 real readings, plans, 3 band renders with 10-13 parts, 4 solo renders), `T5ProbeTest` (126 readings). Plans printed in `plan-*.txt`.

Good: no clipping (peak 0.56-0.63 solo, 0.58-0.90 band, 0 clipped samples); 260-bar part plans in 38 ms, band planning 4-7 ms; empty input, rests-only and bpm 20/400 do not crash; tempo smooth: adjacent-bar change at most 12% except fermatas (a whole-note fermata roughly doubles its bar, by design); last bar broad rall; mean length at a set bpm only 1.6% slow (max 4.5%); band 1-second rms swings 3.5-7.6x, which is phrase/section dynamics, not pumping. Ties held, legato flags set for touching slurred notes, staccato clamped 70-320 ms.

Problems a musician would hear:
- **T5-P1 · S2 · A phrase ends at every rest of a beat or more.** A rhythm figure ("[0.79 0.60]" every bar, 24K Magic bars 12-15, 28-32) becomes a one-bar phrase with its own arch, a let-go on the last note, a 3.5% ease and a breath after it, every bar: tempo x1.04 in each of those bars, the music pulses. Trumpet 1 has 40 phrases in 49 bars, Alto Sax 1 51 in 52; across 126 parts 0.40 phrases per bar, 770 phrases are one beat long. Suggest: a rest ends a phrase only after a line of about two bars or a slur/section end, and a repeated figure should not re-ease.
- **T5-P2 · S2 · Hairpins across a system break are lost.** Maryland School Songs bars 83-84, "p ... cresc (x 2716-3372) ... ff": the hairpin's x range belongs to the end of the staff, the next bar is on the next system (x near 313), so no note falls in it and the "past it" step never fires; notes read 0.55 0.53 0.57 0.52 (flat, falling) into the ff. 21 of 123 sampled hairpins are cut at a barline and continued; a two-bar hairpin recorded in both bars adds 0.16 twice. Resolve hairpins by bar index and quarters, not by x.
- **T5-P3 · S3 · Narrow shape on unmarked parts.** With no dynamics read (24K Magic Trumpet 1, Alto 1: none), notes sit 0.57-0.79; the phrase arch is about 0.09 of velocity, so it plays as a flat line with slight beat weighting. Most scanned dynamics are missing from the readings, so "it ignores my dynamics" will be the experience.
- **T5-P4 · S3 · Accents too strong.** Accent adds 0.24 and marcato 0.30 to the level; at forte (0.82) that is 1.05-1.14, then tones() multiplies by 1.1 (1.2-1.25, cap 1.5). In Ain't It Fun nearly every accented note is near the top; reduce to about +0.12/+0.18 or cap at 1.0 before the 1.1.
- **T5-P5 · S3 · Tempo words never act on real data.** Across 126 real readings the only texts are titles/arrangers and 3 "cresc": no rit., a tempo or accel. were read. The rit./a tempo logic is untested on real music, and `tempoWord` matches only text that starts with rit/rall/accel, so "poco rit.", "molto rit.", "poco a poco accel." do nothing; a rit. with no "a tempo" stays 32% slow to the end.
- **T5-P6 · S4 · Fermatas on rests are ignored** (only `Note.articulations` is checked); 36 fermata notes in 126 parts.
- **T5-P7 · S4 · 243 of 2332 tied notes (10%) have a next note that is not the same pitch or not touching** (misread ties, ties over a repeat jump): they sound as slurred, re-struck notes; harmless, but suggests ties are over-read.

## Clean view: what is wrong, element by element

Evidence: clean/digital-diva-system0.png, digital-maryland-system2.png (top print, bottom clean), scan-timewarp-system0.png and 2.png.
Born-digital Maryland (bass clef) is the closest: staff lines, clef, key, noteheads, stems, ties, accidentals and the rehearsal box come out near the print. Differences:
- Barlines are about 3x as heavy as printed.
- Rehearsal box "B" has its frame cut at the left by the clef side and sits lower than print.
- Slurs and ties are plain arcs with blunt ends (print: crescent, tapering); one tie loses its right end.
- Accidentals (flat, natural, sharp) are heavier and slightly larger.
Born-digital Diva (treble):
- Bars in doubt (7 of 41) stay as print in an orange frame, so bar 1 is print and the rest is redraw: two styles in one line.
- Dynamics: "ff" and "fff" overlap the hairpin between them (black smear); ".mf" gains a stray mark to its left; the long hairpin under bar 1 is several overlapping thin lines, not two converging lines.
- Accent ">" marks are a different size and drift upward; beams are heavier; the triplet "3" bracket moves onto the notes.
- Text ("Arr. Levi Downing", "Perc. Arr. Michael Glasser") is printed twice, offset by a few pixels: the kept outline is drawn over print the paper did not cover; it reads as bold doubled text.
- Rehearsal box "1" has an open corner; the double bar is two equal heavy lines (print: thin and thick); some repeat dots are gone.
Scan (Time Warp): redrawn staff far heavier than the grey scan lines; clef/key missing in some cleaned bars; printed bar numbers ("22", "41") collide with redrawn ones; notes sit above their staff position; bars not Sure remain scan (see T5-04 for the possible misplacement).
Summary: strokes too heavy and uniform (barlines, beams, stems, staff, hairpins), text not redrawn but doubled, dynamics and hairpins collide, clef/key/time reproduced inconsistently. It reads as a different typeface laid over the same page, which is the "looks like Scratch" complaint.
