## T2 - reading and playing a song (tester T2, 2026-10-08)

Method: the real desktop app driven by `review/T2*Test` (worktree `C:\PDF Draw App\.claude\worktrees\agent-a8a666340bc503f7a\sheets-desktop\src\test\kotlin\com\inksheets\desktop\review\`), a library of copies of real PEP BAND parts (Party Medley 4pp landscape, 24K Magic, Toxic portrait, Master of Puppets 2pp, MSOM Shorts 5pp; a 5-song setlist), Windows touch via SimulatedTouch, stylus through PenInput (reflection). Screenshots: `sheets-desktop/build/t2-shots/` in the worktree. Simulation limits: keys go through the same KeyBindingStore table as Main.kt (the window's onKeyEvent is not part of AppRoot); trackpad simulated with horizontal scroll events only (no AWT preciseWheelRotation); bookmarks write on a background thread so the test could not confirm them; night mode checked from code. Wall times include ~37 ms of screenshot per frame.

Numbers: tap or pedal to new page state 20-120 ms; a turn then animates 260 ms (slide + fade, first ~110 ms dim) and is settled after 288 ms. Song step in a set: 336-464 ms to settle with ~14 frames (~220 ms) near-black between songs (150 ms fade out, then fade in); stepping back to an already-read song 128-144 ms. Part switch (this song only): 288 ms animation, 965 ms wall incl. the menu tap. A 5-song setlist opens its first song in about 1.0 s.

### Findings

**T2-01 - S2** - Taps and mouse clicks during the 260 ms page-turn animation are mis-aimed. Three right-third finger taps 1, 3 or 8 frames apart gave pages 1,2,1 (settled back on page 1); mouse clicks gave 2,1,1; the pedal (NEXT_PAGE) gave 1,2,3 correctly; taps 20 frames apart were fine. Cause (code): `EditorScreen.kt:1943` puts `translationX = turnDir * p * width * 0.45` on the DocumentCanvas modifier, so the pointer coordinates its tap handler sees are shifted while the page slides; a right-third tap lands in the middle or left third. Expected: a quick second tap turns on. Evidence: `T2Round2Test` "rapid taps and double taps". Status: open.

**T2-02 - S3** - Desktop and Android disagree on tap/flick rules. Tap time limit 300 ms on desktop (`DocumentCanvas.kt TAP_MS`) vs 450 ms on Android (`DrawingView TAP_TURN_MS`): measured on desktop, a 250 ms press turns, 400 ms and 640 ms do nothing, silently. A diagonal flick (dx 300, dy 250) is ignored on desktop (needs dx > 1.5 dy) but turns on Android (|dx| > |dy|); (dx 300, dy 150) turns on both. Status: open.

**T2-03 - S3** - With the tools out (All tools) and zoomed in, a one-finger sideways flick on desktop turns the page (and refits, losing the zoom); on Android `turnsWithFinger()` requires tools away, so the finger pans. PREFERENCES: "Opening tools keeps pan/zoom". Measured: tools out, pinch, finger flick 15% left: page 2 -> 3. (`EditorScreen.kt:1996` says this is deliberate; it disagrees with Android and with the rule.) Status: open.

**T2-04 - S3** - Tools away and zoomed in by a pinch: a one-finger vertical drag does not move the page at all on desktop (picture identical before and after a 40%-of-height drag), where Android scrolls a zoomed page up and down. Two-finger pan works. A sideways finger never pans at zoom: correct (picture identical after a slow 15% drag). Evidence: `T2Round8Test` "eraser trap, vertical drag at zoom". Status: open.

**T2-05 - S2** - "Half a page on / back" is wrong on a page that already fits. On a fitted landscape page HALF_PAGE_FORWARD pushes the page up ~220 px (blank edge, screenshot `r3t-01`) and returns true; a second press returns false and `Perform.run` turns that into NEXT_SONG, so in a set the second half-page press skips the song. The page refits only on the next page turn. Expected: on a page that fits, act as next page, never jump song. Code: `EditorScreen.kt` HALF_PAGE_* uses `panBy` and returns `offset != before`. Status: open.

**T2-06 - S3** - Space, Enter, N/P do nothing as page keys (only PageDown/Right/Down and PageUp/Left/Up; Home/End first/last; Ctrl+PageDown/Up song). Space is the usual "next page" for a laptop on a stand. Escape with tools away brings the tools back and a second press CLOSES the song (`navigation.back`: immersive -> leave()): two stray Escapes during a gig close the song. Status: open.

**T2-07 - S2** - The Eraser switch leaves the stylus an eraser. Stylus writes (1 stroke); tap strip Eraser on, tap it off; stylus stroke adds 0 (1 -> 1). The strip lights nothing for the stylus, so the player writes and nothing appears. Pen on/off restores it (the next stroke appeared). Design says "stylus keeps its last tool", but turning the switch OFF should give the stylus its previous tool back, or the strip must show what the stylus holds. Evidence: `T2Round8Test`. Status: open.

**T2-08 - S3** - sheets-ui panels break "X top-right + swipe down on every panel". `FloatingPanel` (Tuner, Metronome, Recordings, Together, Notes...) has an X but its header drag MOVES the panel; a 200 px swipe down on the Tuner header left it open. `SheetDialog` (non-movable dialogs) has no X at all, only a text Close or outside tap; neither uses `PanelTop`. Code: `FloatingPanel.kt`, `SheetsHome.kt:717`. Status: open.

**T2-09 - S3** - The strip's Part menu cannot choose "All instruments" (full score for every song); it lists only profiles (Trombone, Baritone / Euphonium, Bass Guitar) and "Select instrument...". Home has "All instruments". The menu is a bare dropdown (no X) covering the top right of the page. "Back to Trombone's part" does appear after a one-song pick. Status: open.

**T2-10 - S2** - Night / Sepia / Greyscale / High contrast live only in the app bar's overflow menu, which exists only with All tools open, and `pageFilter` is `remember`ed per editor (`EditorScreen.kt:273`): not saved, not shared by the songs of a set, reset on every open. A pit or stage player must open All tools, find the menu, pick Night, close tools, on every song. (From code; the screen run could not read the result.) Expected: one remembered setting, reachable from the strip's More. Status: open.

**T2-11 - S3** - Right-click on a set tab offers Close, Close other tabs, Close all tabs (`r5t-03`), though set tabs deliberately have no X. Closing one leaves a hole in the set (open files 5 -> 4, playing unchanged). Expected: no per-song close on set tabs, or "Close the set". Status: open.

**T2-12 - S3** - Duplicate controls with All tools open: three Undo (app bar, strip, toolbar), strip Pen vs toolbar Draw, strip "Hide tools" beside the toolbar's own More. Violates "no duplicate control sets". On a phone the page is covered by strip plus toolbar (`r7-phone-04`). Status: open.

**T2-13 - S3** - Phone portrait with the strip on the LEFT: the strip overlays the left edge of the page (Metronome / Tuner icons over the music, `r7-phone-02`); strip right keeps a lane. PREFERENCES: nothing is drawn over the music. Status: open.

**T2-14 - S4** - The strip is open by default on a first open (page not centred: 165-1370 of 1600); PREFERENCES says no bar until summoned. After the first centre tap it stays folded. Consider default folded, or a one-time hint. Status: open.

**T2-15 - S3** - Song change in a set fades the old song to near-black and the new one in: ~14 frames (~220 ms) near-black, 336-464 ms to settle; longest on the first step after opening the set (read-ahead starts 700 ms after a turn; stepping sooner reads the song on demand). Back to a read song 128-144 ms. Page turns settle at 288 ms with the first ~110 ms dim. No blank paper frame and no freeze seen. MobileSheets feels instant: consider a shorter or no fade by default. Evidence: `T2Round4Test`, `T2Round2Test`. Status: open.

**T2-16 - S4** - End of set is silent: a right tap or pedal on the last page of the last song does nothing, nor left on page 1 of song 1. Stepping always starts the new song at page 1 (even if left on its last page), good. A song opened from Home resumes its last page and camera (`ReadingPosition`), with no "start at page 1" option. Status: open.

**T2-17 - S4** - Two song steps in opposite directions with no gap lose one (6 alternating steps from song 5 ended on song 4): `step()` reads `playing`, which `followInSet` rewrites asynchronously. Possible with watch flicks. Status: open.

**T2-18 - S4** - Trackpad: two-finger sideways swipes under ~2.5 "notches" total do nothing (1.0 and 2.0 no turn; 4.0 and 12.0 one page; 1.2 s of coasting still one page; two swipes 400 ms apart two pages). Sensible, but the threshold is in untested precision-touchpad units: needs a real trackpad pass. Wheel or Ctrl+wheel zooms whenever tools are out or the page is zoomed. Status: open.

**T2-19 - S4** - Highlighter is not on the default strip (only Pen and Eraser), though PREFERENCES says Pen/Highlighter/Eraser are one tap away. Status: open.

### Solid
- Thirds: left third back, right third on (.30 and .68 inside, .62 is the centre); middle folds/unfolds the strip without moving the page; bottom-middle brings All tools; middle again closes and refits; taps on strip buttons never turn.
- A wiggle up to 10 px keeps a tap; 14 px and more is no tap (12 dp rule); a flick starting on the strip lane does not turn; out-and-back without lifting does not turn.
- Mouse: click right/left, middle opens strip, drag across turns, wheel notch turns one page. Keys via the table: PageDown/PageUp/Arrows, Home, End, Ctrl+PageDown/Up, Ctrl +/-/0.
- Stylus: tap never turns; with tools away it writes; a finger with tools away never writes; strip Pen lets the finger write (a finger tap then writes nothing and does not turn); strip Undo and pedal Undo work; Eraser switch lets a finger erase.
- Setlist: strip "Song n/5", tabs in set order without X, Home closes all tabs and ends the set, steps always start at page 1.
- Strip right/left, Part menu and All tools at phone, phone-land, phone-small-land, tablet10, tablet10-land, laptop-short: 0 UiAudit problems (the "Close sheet" scrim reported for the Pages sheet is a false overlap).
